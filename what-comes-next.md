# What comes next

Dynamic Hooks ships in an order, not on a calendar. Each step starts when the one before it has been checked. No step has a date, and none will be given one until it is done.

1. **This document and the token.** This document, the site, and $DHOOKS on Robinhood Chain.
2. **The build.** The contracts in [Under the hood](under-the-hood.md), tested against a copy of Robinhood Chain and on its public testnet. Review of the hook, the program registry, the escrow and the lock, with findings published. Deployment behind the launch pause, exercised with the team's own money on real markets, every contract verified, every address published.
3. **Markets open.** ETH-quoted markets from the Person, Creator and Milestone templates. Rules, holders pools, referral links and the first verifier live from the first market.
4. **Quote in a stock.** The allowed list opens with the first Robinhood Stock Tokens.
5. **Votes and weight.** `Vote` as a condition. $DHOOKS staking for holders-pool weight, written up on its own and reviewed before it ships.

What is not on this list is on the [What cannot change](what-cannot-change.md) page.
