# Straight talk

**Markets can go to zero.** A market on Dynamic Hooks is a token with locked liquidity and a program. The program changes economics; it does not create demand. Most markets anywhere end at zero.

**Rules are only as good as their conditions.** A `Verified` rule trusts a verifier's signature. A `State` rule trusts the chain. We publish the allow-list and the code, and we run the first verifier ourselves. If you would not trust us to sign, do not launch a market that depends on our signature.

**Verification is not endorsement.** A creator proving a handle means the money goes to them. It does not mean they asked for the market, like it, or will ever acknowledge it.

**Stock-quoted markets inherit their issuer's rules.** Who can hold a Stock Token, and where, is decided by its issuer, not by this protocol.

**$DHOOKS comes first.** The token comes before the protocol. Its price is set by a market. Buybacks are how the protocol spends part of its revenue, not a floor under anything. Until markets open, holding $DHOOKS is holding a position in the plan written here, and plans can fail.

**No one from this project will ever message you first,** ask for a seed phrase, or send a claim link. The only addresses that matter are on the [Addresses](addresses.md) page.
