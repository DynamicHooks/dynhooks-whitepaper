# Introduction

**Dynamic Hooks is the programmable market layer on Robinhood Chain.**

Launch a market. Program its economics. Let it evolve on-chain.

Every launchpad so far asks you to set the rules once, at launch, and live with them. A fee is a number. A fee recipient is an address. Liquidity is locked or it isn't. Nothing changes after the first block, no matter what happens to the market or the people behind it.

Dynamic Hooks is built on a different premise: **a market's rules should respond to what the market does and to what can be proven about it.** A creator verifies their identity, and fee routing changes. Liquidity crosses a threshold, and the fee drops. A milestone is attested, and rewards switch on. An agent proves a condition, and a predefined rule fires. All of it inside the swap, enforced by a Uniswap v4 hook, with hard limits that no rule can cross.

This document explains what a market is here, how its program works, who gets paid, what can and cannot change, and what the $DHOOKS token does. It has no dates in it. Things ship in an order, and each step is announced when it is done.
